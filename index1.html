Hi, I'm Erika — a graphic designer and video editor based in Barcelona. I work at the intersection of brand identity, editorial design, and visual storytelling. I believe good design is invisible: it guides, communicates, and moves people without them realising why.

Whether it's a brand system from scratch, a magazine layout, a social campaign, or a short film — I bring the same care to every pixel and every frame.
